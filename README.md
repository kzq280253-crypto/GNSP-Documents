# GNSP Official Documents Repository

**Global Nexus Simulation Platform (GNSP) — Official Documents**

This repository hosts the official governing documents of the GNSP, established under United Nations General Assembly resolution **A/RES/84/206** (27 September 2029) and UNESCO resolution **44 C/Res. 118** (14 June 2028).

Official documents:

- `A_RES_84_206.md` — UN General Assembly resolution establishing GNSP (27 Sep 2029)
- `UNESCO_44C_Res_118.md` — UNESCO 44th General Conference resolution (14 Jun 2028)
- `Singapore_Framework_Agreement.md` — Founding framework agreement signed in Singapore (15 Nov 2027)

Related repositories: [GNSP-Platform](https://github.com/kzq280253-crypto/GNSP-Platform) (main project) · [GNSP-Newsroom](https://github.com/kzq280253-crypto/GNSP-Newsroom) (press releases)
