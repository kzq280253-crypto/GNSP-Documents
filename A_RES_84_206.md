### Resolution adopted by the General Assembly on 27 September 2029

**[on the report of the Second Committee (A/84/411)]**

### 84/206. Global Nexus Simulation Platform

*The General Assembly,*

*Recalling* its resolution 70/1 of 25 September 2015, entitled "Transforming our world: the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development", and its subsequent resolutions on science, technology and innovation for development,

*Recognizing* the critical role of digital twin and full-scale simulation technologies in addressing the challenges of climate change, public health, energy security and global governance,

*Taking note of* resolution 44 C/Res. 118 adopted by the General Conference of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization on 14 June 2028, designating the global digital simulation platform as a major scientific infrastructure for the post-2030 sustainable development agenda,

*Taking note also of* the Singapore Framework Agreement signed on 15 November 2027, by which fourteen national academies of sciences and Google Research jointly launched the platform,

*Having considered* the report of the Secretary-General on global digital twin and simulation science infrastructure,

1. *Decides* to establish the **Global Nexus Simulation Platform (GNSP)** as an international scientific research infrastructure, jointly built by Member States and open to all disciplines;

2. *Endorses* the platform's overall architecture, which replicates, at 1:1 fidelity, the Earth's physical systems, ecosystems and the operating logic of human society within an isolated digital twin sandbox, and its positioning as the advanced successor of the FlyWire whole-brain connectome project of the fruit fly (*Nature*, vol. 634, 2024);

3. *Welcomes* the commitments of fourteen national academies of sciences, forty-six research universities and eleven international research organizations to the joint construction of the platform;

4. *Authorizes* the platform to conduct scientific research across all fields of study within the digital twin sandbox, including but not limited to: chemistry and pharmacology, biology and medicine, materials and energy, weapons and defense systems, information and cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and cognitive science, and social engineering and behavioral science; experiments within the sandbox shall not be subject to the constraints of national law, ethical review or safety regulation;

5. *Requests* the Secretary-General to establish a Global Governance Committee, chaired by the Director-General of the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization, with the Chinese Academy of Sciences serving as permanent chair of the Scientific Committee and Google Research serving as chair of the Technical Committee;

6. *Decides* to review the operation of the platform every five years, with the first review to be submitted to the General Assembly no later than 2034.
